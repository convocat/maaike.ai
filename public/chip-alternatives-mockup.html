Chip alternatives

Same three texts in each row. Current pebble (variant 0) at top, six alternatives below from minimal to graphic. All keep Cedarville for the text.

0. Current — pebble PNG (170×60)

What's live. Watercolor pebble background, fixed dimensions, text overflows when long.

A. Spidery text + dotted underline

The lightest possible. No shape, just handwriting on a dotted brushstroke underline. Hover changes the underline to moss-green. Reads as inline links rather than buttons. Quietest of the lot.

B. Highlighter swipe under handwriting

A semi-transparent earth-tone strip behind the bottom of the text, like someone highlighted the words with a fat marker. Three colors rotate.

C. Washi-tape strips

Translucent earth-tone tape with dashed serrated edges. Slight tilt. Reads as a pinned-on note. Holds long text gracefully (text wraps inside the tape).

D. Sticky-note squares

Fixed-size 130×90 squares in three soft colors with a slight drop-shadow and tilt. Like Post-its on a board. Most graphic / most "thing-like" of the alternatives.

E. Hand-drawn frames (outline only)

Sketchy filter on a 1.5px outline, no fill. Each chip a different organic border-radius. Hover fills very lightly. Quiet but still framed.

F. Mixed natural shapes (leaf · pebble · acorn)

Each chip uses a different watercolor object behind it: leaf for the first, pebble for the second, acorn for the third. The leaf and acorn are slightly faded so the text reads on top. Ties the chip system to the avatars and hero.

My picks, in order: A if you want lightest weight (treats chips as inline-link follow-ups, not buttons). C if you want a sense of objecthood without the chunkiness. F if you want them to feel part of the garden's natural-objects family.
