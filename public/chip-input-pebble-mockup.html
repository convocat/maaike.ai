C. Pebbles as a heap (hover to surface one)

Pebbles overlap into a small pile, like stones tossed on a beach. Hover or focus a pebble and it lifts and rotates upright, bringing its caption with it. The fourth (input) pebble is in the heap too. Works at any width because it doesn't depend on horizontal space.
