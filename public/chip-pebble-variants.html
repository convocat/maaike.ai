Same three texts. Current pebble (0) at top, six pebble-specific alternatives below. The pebbles get smaller / move around / become accents rather than full backgrounds.

0. Current — pebble fills the chip

What's live. Pebble is 170×60 behind the text.

A. Caption style — small pebble, text below

The pebble shrinks to ~70×26, sits as a small painted shape with the question written underneath like a caption.

B. Tiny pebble bullet + inline text

Pebble becomes a small painted bullet (~28×14) before the text. Reads more like a list item than a button. Text is the main thing.

C. Faded pebble halo behind text

Pebble grows larger but drops to 45% opacity, becoming a soft watercolor halo. Text reads on top in full strength.

D. Pebble + text in a pinned label

Small pebble paired with the text, inside a translucent rectangle with a dashed border (like a pinned-on note from a notebook).

E. Pebble cluster (a path of three small stones)

Above each chip, three tiny pebbles laid in a row like a path. The text follows. Reads as "follow this thread".

F. Pebble with shadow, text below — like a stone resting on paper

A 100×36 pebble with a soft drop-shadow as if sitting on a surface, text written on the surface beneath. Reads three-dimensional.

G. Pebble overlap — text spills below the bottom edge

Pebble at the top, smaller (150×50), with the text starting partway through the pebble and continuing below it. Long text gets natural breathing room.
