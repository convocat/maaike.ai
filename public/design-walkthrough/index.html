Design walkthrough GenAI mortgage assistant brief

The brief

A major Canadian bank is piloting a GenAI-powered mortgage pre-approval assistant. Unlike a chatbot, this system works largely in the background. It pulls the applicant's financial documents, cross-references income, liabilities, and credit data, and progressively builds a pre-approval picture without requiring the user to sit in a conversation.

The system only surfaces to the user when it hits something it cannot resolve autonomously: a data inconsistency, a missing document, or a decision point that needs human confirmation.

The design challenge: the moments the system surfaces to the user.

Constraints that matter

  • The user hasn't been in an active conversation. They submitted an application 20 minutes ago and got on with their day. The AI interrupting them is an intrusion.
  • Low-to-moderate AI literacy. They don't know what the system has been doing.
  • Enormous stakes. Likely the largest financial decision of their life.
  • The system cannot make commitments or say "you're approved."
  • Regulatory context limits certain language.
  • There's a human handoff somewhere. Where and how is part of the problem.

What I'd love to see

Not a finished flow, but your thinking process:

  • How do you frame what this system is to a user who didn't sign up for a chatbot?
  • What does the interruption moment look and sound like across channels?
  • How do you design for trust when the user has no visibility into what the AI has been doing?
  • Where is the human handoff, and how do you design that transition without it feeling like failure?
  • And the question I'm most curious about: is "conversation" even the right interaction model here, and if not, what is?
