Why this design (not the earlier mockups)

  • The need: when the bot says "Maaike argues X in [post Y]", the reader wants to verify or skim Y without leaving the chat. Today they'd open Y in a new tab and lose the conversation.
  • The mechanism: cited posts arrive as tabs in the right panel. The current page is always there. Hovering a chat citation highlights the relevant paragraph.
  • What it doesn't do: duplicate the chat's content as cards (variant A did this). Show structured triples that only Maaike cares about (variant B). Try to do everything (variant C).
  • Mobile / narrow: right panel hides; chat behaves exactly as today.
