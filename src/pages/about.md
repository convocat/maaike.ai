I'm Maaike. I work at the intersection of conversation design, voice interfaces, and AI. This is my digital garden: a non-linear, associative collection of ideas, notes, articles, and projects at various stages of growth.

Unlike a blog, nothing here is sorted by date. Ideas link to each other, evolve over time, and sometimes get composted when they've run their course. If you're used to scrolling a feed, this might feel different. Try clicking something that catches your eye and see where it takes you.

## What grows here

- **Articles:** longer thinking pieces, often migrated from Substack or written here
- **Field notes:** observations and reflections from working with AI
- **Seeds:** ideas waiting to germinate
- **Experiments:** hands-on explorations you can try yourself
- **Videos:** talks, tutorials, and recordings
- **Library:** books I've read, am reading, or want to read
- **Weblinks:** commentary on external content

## How maturity works

Each piece has a maturity indicator showing how developed it is:

- 🌱 **Seedling:** just getting started
- 🌿 **Sprout:** taking shape, being worked out
- 🪴 **Plant:** solid, well-formed
- 🌳 **Tree:** finished, stable

## Connections

Content is cross-linked with wiki-links. You'll see backlinks at the bottom of posts, related items in the sidebar, and suggested reading from the library. The [explore page](/explore) is an interactive map of everything in the garden, organized into territories by topic.

## AI transparency

Some content here is generated with AI assistance. Every piece carries an indicator:

- **100% Maai:** fully written by me, no AI
- **Assisted:** my ideas and structure, AI helped refine
- **Co-created:** generated by AI based on my ideas and direction
- **Generated:** fully AI-generated, reviewed by me

## Projects

Larger threads of work are organized as projects, each with a hub page and related files. You can find them on the [projects page](/projects).
