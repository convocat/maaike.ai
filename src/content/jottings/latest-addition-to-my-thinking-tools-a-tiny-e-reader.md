Latest addition to my Thinking Tools collection: a tiny e-reader 💜

Sounds relatable? Grabbing my phone has become such a primary reflex that 99% of the times, I can't resist and drift off into mindlessness. And I hate it, because I know I like reading so much better.

Enter the XTEink X4, an [E Ink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E_Ink) e-reader that fits in my hand, and is so cute that I just want to hold it all the time. The reading experience is great, especially after flashing it with open source Crosspoint reader.

And most importantly: it goes with me everywhere. With considerable success so far: my book-to-phone ratio is on in favor of books again 🤗
