Dear Convofriends!

I'm so happy to reconnect with you all, and yesterday's Convoclub really left me energised. So I thought, why not try a newsletter of some sorts. Probably going to be highly irregular, but hey, let's go with the flow.

Over the last few weeks, I've created www.maaike.ai, a digital garden and a playground for my adventures in human thinking & writing and generative AI. It's also where I publish my latest articles and jottings.

## From the garden

**[[is-conversation-still-a-useful-metaphor|Is conversation still a useful metaphor for human-machine interaction?]]**
Up till now, we've quietly assumed that, just because it mimics frictionless interaction between humans, conversation is the best interaction model for human-machine interaction as well. I'm exploring different perspectives; this article shows thoughts and ideas on alternative metaphors.

**[[a-digital-garden-as-central-space|A digital garden as central space for my thoughts and writing]]**
One thing that I really miss doing lately, is writing longer pieces that require a bit of thinking, mulling and reordering. I typically resort to videos nowadays, and, to be honest, Claude is a pretty mean writing assistant for factual, functional texts. But I really miss sitting in front of a screen that doesn't talk back, doesn't distract and just offers me space for my own head.

**[[garden-as-metaphor]]**
I really liked the idea of a place where things are allowed to be messy, unfinished, and in progress. And after about three weeks, I find this metaphor not only holds really well, but also gives me inspiration for extending it for separate parts of my website.

## What's in the news

[Designing for agentic AI: practical UX patterns for control, consent, and accountability](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2026/02/designing-agentic-ai-practical-ux-patterns/) — Smashing Magazine on UX patterns for agentic AI: how do you design for consent and accountability when AI acts on your behalf?

[The arrival of AI demands a new epistemic paradigm](https://etcjournal.com/2026/03/28/the-arrival-of-ai-demands-a-new-epistemic-paradigm/) — argues we need a distributed epistemology that assigns credit and responsibility across human-AI networks, not just individual minds.

[The internet's hidden creative renaissance](https://newpublic.substack.com/p/the-handmade-internet-is-making-a) — people building personal, intentional sites again as a quiet rebellion against algorithmic platforms.

## Seed of thought

Funny, isn't it: for years, buttons were a no-go in chatbots: we should design voice first, natural language, conversational interaction. And lo and behold: Anthropic is reviving good old buttons-and-IVR! Much to my own delight, there's nothing wrong with some solid, deterministic routing.

## What I'm reading

*Bacteria to AI* by [N. Katherine Hayles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N._Katherine_Hayles). This book is something to savour slowly and deliberately. It's giving me so much new vocabulary for describing what I experience when I work with AI: definitely not embodied or conscious, yet weirdly cognitive and attuned.
