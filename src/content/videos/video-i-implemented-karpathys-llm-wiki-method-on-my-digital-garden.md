# Video: I implemented Karpathy's LLM wiki method on my digital garden

Andrej Karpathy made some waves this week with his idea of an [LLM Wiki](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f), where the core idea is that "The idea here is different. Instead of just retrieving from raw documents at query time, the LLM incrementally builds and maintains a persistent wiki, a structured, interlinked collection of markdown files that sits between you and the raw sources."

This of course, is exactly what [maaike.ai](https://www.maaike.ai), my [[garden-as-metaphor|digital garden]], has been about all the time already, with one big exception: my garden contains my thoughts and ideas, in my own words.

I rebuilt Karpathy's LLM wiki on top of my content, with interesting results!
