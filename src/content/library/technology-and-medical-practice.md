A video-based study of how the medical record, on paper and on screen, is used inside the consultation between doctor and patient. The argument runs parallel to [[technology-in-action|Technology in Action]]: a technology's use and meaning are produced through the situated, moment-by-moment interaction around it, not delivered by the design. A concrete, workplace-grounded companion to the book.

Published 2003 in Sociology of Health & Illness, volume 25, issue 3, pp. 75 to 96 (Blackwell). Open-access copy via the DiVA institutional repository.
