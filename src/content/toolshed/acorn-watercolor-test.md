A quick visual sanity check for the watercolor acorn illustration (`/images/watercolor-acorn-trimmed.png`). The mockup tiles the same image across three backgrounds: pure white, pale moss `#DDE6D0`, and near-black `#2A332A`. The point is to see how the trimmed edges read against the [[color tokens]] used elsewhere on the site, both light and dark mode contexts.

The acorn is a candidate decorative element for headers or empty states. This test answers a simple question: does the edge halo from PNG trimming survive on each background, or does it ring around the illustration on the dark tile.
