A typography study for the line of metadata under a post title (published date, tended date, maturity stage, AI attribution). The mockup compares two formats applied to four real post stubs from the garden.

Option A renders each item as a small pill with an explicit label and value inside it. Option B keeps everything on a single inline line: label, value, separator, next label, next value. Both options share the same content so the difference is purely visual language.
