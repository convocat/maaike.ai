The framework emphasizes faithful reproduction of content (matching ingredients, portions, and identity) through human ground truth anchors, multimodal routing, cross-modal verification, QA gates with retry logic, production drift monitoring, and iterative tuning before deployment.

themes:
- Production AI systems require multi-layered evaluation gates that verify faithfulness across modalities, not just aesthetic quality improvement.
- AI enhancement without constraint risks 'pixel slop' that preserves appearance while corrupting semantic content, requiring evaluation at the work level rather than model level.
- Closed-loop feedback systems where evaluation failures inform generation retries create adaptive systems that resist drift while maintaining fidelity to ground truth.
- Human labeling of ground truth data, comprehensive logging, and continuous sampling from production environments are essential infrastructure for trustworthy AI agent systems.
