# GenAI

**Type:** entity
**First seen:** 2023 (is-conversation-still-a-useful-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
GenAI (generative AI) is the broader category of AI systems capable of generating text, images, code, and other content from a prompt. Maaike uses GenAI as the category label when her argument extends beyond text-based LLMs to the full scope of AI-generated content. The conversation metaphor, she argues, breaks down specifically for GenAI — not just for chatbots.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike distinguishes between the older chatbot paradigm (scripted, task-scoped) and GenAI (open-ended, generative). The conversation metaphor was already imperfect for chatbots; it becomes actively misleading for GenAI, which is asked to do things conversations don't do (generate long documents, produce code, synthesise research). The garden article also notes that GenAI risks suppressing thinking-in-public — generating polished outputs instead of exposing the thinking process.
