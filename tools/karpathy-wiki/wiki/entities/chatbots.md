**Type:** technology-category
**First seen:** 2023 (an-evening-with-chatgpt)

## Summary
Chatbots are software systems that simulate conversation, ranging from rule-based systems to LLM-powered generative AI. Maaike's work spans the pre-GenAI and post-GenAI chatbot landscape: she built and studied rule-based chatbots professionally before GenAI, which gives her comparative perspective on what changed and what didn't when LLMs replaced intent-NLU architectures.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike uses "chatbots" to refer to the older, rule-based generation of conversational software — in contrast to LLM-based systems like ChatGPT. Her professional background includes designing chatbots using NLU intent architectures, which makes her sensitive to what the transition to generative AI actually changed (and what it didn't). The conversation metaphor and its problems predate GenAI — chatbots without AI were already hard to design well.
