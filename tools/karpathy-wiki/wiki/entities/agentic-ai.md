# Agentic AI

**Type:** entity
**First seen:** 2023 (is-conversation-still-a-useful-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Agentic AI refers to AI systems that can autonomously plan and execute multi-step tasks, often using tools and external services. Maaike invokes agentic AI as the paradigm that makes the delegation metaphor more appropriate than the conversation metaphor — when an AI is executing a task over time, the interaction model is closer to briefing a contractor than having a chat.

## How Maaike uses this concept
The delegation metaphor better fits agentic AI because agentic systems are not primarily about turn-taking exchange but about task assignment and deliverable production. Maaike uses this to argue that interface designers need to move beyond conversational UI patterns when designing for agentic contexts.
