# ChatGPT

**Type:** entity
**First seen:** 2022 (why-chatgpt-is-bullshit-and-why-we-should-design-for-that)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
ChatGPT is OpenAI's conversational AI product, released in late 2022. It is the primary instance Maaike uses when making her broader arguments about LLMs: that they are structurally indifferent to truth, violate Gricean cooperative norms, and activate anthropomorphism through their conversational form. ChatGPT functions in her writing both as the specific subject of critique and as the representative case for all LLM-based chat interfaces.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike writes extensively about ChatGPT through direct engagement: she has published multiple "an evening with ChatGPT" articles documenting her interactions. Her analysis moves from observation to structural argument — what she observes in ChatGPT's behavior is explained by the underlying mechanism (probabilistic word prediction without ground truth access). ChatGPT is not uniquely bad; it is the most visible instance of a systemic design problem.
