# Paul Grice

**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Paul Grice was a philosopher of language who formulated the cooperative principle: the implicit agreement between conversational participants to be truthful, informative, relevant, and clear. Maaike uses Grice as the theoretical baseline for what conversational communication presupposes — a baseline that LLMs structurally violate.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike's argument about LLMs as bullshitters rests partly on Grice: when we engage in conversation, we implicitly expect cooperative participation. LLMs perform the surface form of conversation while violating its foundational contract (the maxim of quality — be truthful). This gap between conversational form and cooperative substance is, for Maaike, the core design problem of conversational AI.
