## Summary
Susan Brennan is a cognitive scientist and co-author with Herbert Clark of the "Grounding in Communication" framework. In Maaike's work she appears as part of the Clark-Brennan dyad, jointly credited for the grounding theory that underpins Maaike's critique of the conversation metaphor for AI.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Brennan is cited alongside Clark as the source of conversational grounding theory. The joint attribution signals that Maaike is drawing on established psycholinguistic research rather than informal observation.
