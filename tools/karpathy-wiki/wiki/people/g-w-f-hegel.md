# G W F Hegel

**Type:** person
**First seen:** 2024 (thinking-in-action-precision-matters)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
G W F Hegel was a 19th-century German philosopher and originator of dialectical thinking — the thesis-antithesis-synthesis framework. Maaike invokes Hegel as the originator of a method of reasoning she finds valuable and argues LLMs undermine: the practice of deliberately confronting a position with its opposite to achieve synthesis.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike argues that clear thinking requires dialectical challenge — you have to test your position against its strongest counter-argument. LLMs, she contends, reinforce epistemic bias rather than countering it: they tend to affirm and elaborate rather than genuinely challenge. Hegel's dialectical method is the normative ideal against which LLM behavior is found wanting.
