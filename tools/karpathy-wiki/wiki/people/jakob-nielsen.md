**Type:** person

## Summary
Jakob Nielsen is a usability researcher and co-founder of Nielsen Norman Group. In Maaike's work he appears as the source of key terms for new AI user behaviors: accordion editing, apple picking, and the articulation barrier — concepts Maaike adopts as analytical tools for understanding the usability problems of conversational AI interfaces.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike uses Nielsen's vocabulary to name patterns she observes in how users struggle with conversational AI for writing tasks. The articulation barrier in particular becomes a structuring concept in her argument that conversational interfaces impose new cognitive demands that non-conversational interfaces (direct manipulation) handle better.
