## Summary
Omar Shaikh is a researcher (with Diyi Yang and Dan Jurafsky) who coined or defined the concept of "grounding gap" — the structural failure of LLMs to achieve communicative grounding. Maaike encounters this via library reading and uses it as technical corroboration for her argument about what LLMs lack.

## How Maaike uses this concept
The grounding gap research provides Maaike with a technically grounded (pun intended) description of why LLMs fail at conversational grounding. It complements her more philosophical sources (Clark, Grice) with empirical research.
