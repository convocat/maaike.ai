# Herbert Clark

**Type:** person

## Summary
Herbert Clark is a psycholinguist at Stanford, co-author (with Susan Brennan) of the foundational "Grounding in Communication" framework (1991). Maaike uses Clark's grounding theory to articulate precisely what LLMs lack: the ability to achieve mutual understanding through collaborative conversational acts.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike draws on Clark to show that "conversation" is not just turn-taking — it presupposes grounding, a process of building shared understanding through confirmation and repair. LLMs cannot do this. They produce outputs that look like conversational responses without engaging in the underlying cooperative process. This is why the conversation metaphor is misleading: it activates user expectations (of grounding, of mutual understanding) that LLMs cannot fulfill.
