# Helena Rodemann

**Type:** person
**First seen:** 2023 (is-conversation-still-a-useful-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Helena Rodemann is a design researcher who argues that personality in chat agents constitutes a form of emotional labor. Maaike uses her framework to highlight the ethical and design implications of building relational personas into AI systems.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike connects Rodemann's emotional labor argument to her broader concern about conversation design as a source of anthropomorphism risk. Designing for warmth, personality, and relational tone is not neutral — it shapes how users perceive and relate to the system. Rodemann's framing makes this explicit.
