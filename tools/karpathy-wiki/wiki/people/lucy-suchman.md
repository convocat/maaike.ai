# Lucy Suchman

**Type:** person

## Summary
Lucy Suchman is an anthropologist and computer scientist, author of "Plans and Situated Actions" (1987). Her central argument — that human action is shaped by concrete, unfolding situations rather than pre-formed abstract plans — becomes in Maaike's work a lens for understanding what LLMs cannot do: act situatedly, in the moment, with genuine responsiveness to context.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike invokes Suchman's situated action concept to sharpen the critique of the conversation metaphor. Conversation is not just turn-taking but situated response — each utterance is shaped by the accumulated context of the interaction. LLMs lack this capacity: they process tokens but do not inhabit a situation. This connects to her broader argument about the anthropomorphism risk in conversation design.
