## Summary
Emily Bender is a computational linguist at the University of Washington, co-author (with Timnit Gebru, Angelina McMillan-Major, and Margaret Mitchell) of the 2021 "Stochastic Parrots" paper. Maaike cites her as one of the originators of the stochastic parrot critique, which frames LLMs as pattern-stitching systems without semantic understanding.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike uses the stochastic parrot framing to complement her Frankfurt-based bullshit argument: both point to the same structural feature (absence of truth-tracking), approached from different disciplines. Bender and Gebru's contribution is to frame it in terms of language and meaning, while Frankfurt frames it in terms of epistemic ethics.
