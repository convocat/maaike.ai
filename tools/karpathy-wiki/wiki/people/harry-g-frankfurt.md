# Harry G. Frankfurt

**Type:** person

**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Harry G. Frankfurt is a moral philosopher best known for his 1986 essay "On Bullshit," which defines bullshit as speech produced with indifference to its truth or falsity. Maaike uses Frankfurt's framework as the conceptual spine for her argument that LLMs are structurally bullshitters — not liars, but producers of output that has no relationship to truth at all.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike finds Frankfurt's distinction between lying and bullshitting precise and important. A liar knows the truth and hides it; a bullshitter simply doesn't care. She argues this maps perfectly onto LLMs: they are not "wrong" in the way a lying person is wrong — they are indifferent to the truth-falsity distinction by design. This reframing changes the design response: you cannot correct a bullshitter by pointing out their errors, you have to design around the indifference itself.

## Open questions
- Frankfurt's essay predates LLMs by decades — does his framework hold at scale, or does it need extension for systems that produce billions of outputs?
