## Summary
Don Norman is a cognitive scientist and designer, co-founder of Nielsen Norman Group, and author of "The Design of Everyday Things." In Maaike's work he appears as co-author (with Jakob Nielsen) of the NN/g research on new AI user behaviors — accordion editing and apple picking.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Norman is cited alongside Nielsen as the source of the accordion editing and apple picking terminology. His broader work on affordances and design for human cognition forms an implicit background to Maaike's concern about interfaces that mismatch human cognitive patterns.
