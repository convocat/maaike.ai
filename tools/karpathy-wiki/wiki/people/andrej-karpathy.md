## Summary
Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and former Tesla/OpenAI engineer whose public thinking Maaike engages with directly. He coined the term "context engineering," which Maaike adopts and reframes as "context design." She cites him as a model of dialectical thinking in practice — someone who updates his positions publicly when confronted with counter-evidence.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike positions herself in dialogue with Karpathy rather than simply deferring to him. She takes his term "context engineering" and argues it should be called "context design" — because the work it describes is structurally identical to what information architects and technical authors have done for decades. Karpathy becomes the entry point for her broader argument about designers belonging at the GenAI table.
