## Summary
"Bender and Gebru" is a combined attribution for Emily Bender and Timnit Gebru as co-authors of the 2021 Stochastic Parrots paper. The combined form is retained for backward compatibility with sources that cite them jointly. See individual pages for [[emily-bender]] and [[timnit-gebru]].

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike cites Bender and Gebru's stochastic parrot concept as a characterisation of LLMs that complements her own Frankfurt-based framing. The two arguments converge: an LLM is both a stochastic parrot (no semantic understanding) and a bullshitter (no concern for truth).
