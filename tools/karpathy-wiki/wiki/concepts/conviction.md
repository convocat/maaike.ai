# Conviction

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (thinking-in-action-precision-matters)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Conviction is a firmly held belief that someone considers part of their identity — stronger than opinion, more conscious than a core belief. Maaike uses it to distinguish different types of belief that require different levels of epistemic justification and different treatment in dialectical thinking.
