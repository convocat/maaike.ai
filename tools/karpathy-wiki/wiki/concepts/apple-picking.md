# Apple picking

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (conversational-interfaces-are-not-easy)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Apple picking is Nielsen Norman Group's term for the behavior of selecting pieces of previously generated content to use as context or material for a new prompt — picking the good apples from the pile. Maaike uses it alongside accordion editing as evidence that users develop workaround behaviors to compensate for the limitations of conversational AI interfaces.
