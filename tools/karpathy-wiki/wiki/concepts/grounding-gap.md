# Grounding gap

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (grounding-gaps-in-language-model-generations)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
The grounding gap is the structural failure of LLMs to achieve communicative grounding — coined by Shaikh, Yang, and Jurafsky. LLMs produce outputs that simulate grounded communication while lacking the underlying cooperative process. This technical research concept provides empirical support for Maaike's theoretical argument about what LLMs lack.
