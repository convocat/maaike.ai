# Note-taking metaphor

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (is-conversation-still-a-useful-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
The note-taking metaphor frames AI interaction as an extension of note-taking within a task domain: continuous, marginal, executable — rather than a separate conversation. It frames the AI as a smart notebook integrated into your workflow rather than a separate conversational partner. Maaike offers it alongside delegation and inner dialog as alternatives to the conversation metaphor.

## How Maaike uses this concept
The note-taking metaphor is appealing to Maaike because it preserves the user's agency and cognitive ownership. In a note-taking frame, you are still the author; the AI is the instrument. It connects to her interest in digital gardens and knowledge management — the AI as tool within your thinking practice, not as a separate thinking entity.
