# Designing for doubt

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (why-chatgpt-is-bullshit-and-why-we-should-design-for-that)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Designing for doubt is Maaike's design approach for LLM-based interfaces: building skepticism, source-tracking, and fact-checking capacity into the interface rather than relying on users to bring their own critical faculties. Given that users are cognitively lazy (Grice) and easily anthropomorphize (conversation metaphor), the interface must do some of the epistemic work.

## How Maaike uses this concept
This is Maaike's main design prescription arising from her critique of LLMs. If LLMs are structurally indifferent to truth and users are prone to trust them uncritically, then the design response is not to make LLMs more accurate (a model problem) but to make interfaces that support user skepticism. Designing for doubt counters indifference to truth at the interface level: show sources, flag uncertainty, create friction at high-stakes decisions, make verification easy.
