# Building as specification

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2025 (from-role-to-skills-how-claude-code-desilos-the-future-of-work)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Building as specification is Maaike's insight that the act of building something with an AI tool (especially Claude Code) is itself a form of specification: you discover what you actually want by building it, rather than by writing requirements first. The build process externalizes tacit knowledge in a way that verbal specification often cannot.

## How Maaike uses this concept
This concept emerges from Maaike's direct experience building her digital garden with Claude Code. She observes that traditional requirements → specification → build cycles often fail because people can't articulate what they want until they see it. Building iteratively with AI collapses this cycle: you express requirements through building, not before it. This connects to her interest in how GenAI changes creative and professional workflows.
