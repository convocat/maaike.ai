# Rhetoric

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (thinking-in-action-precision-matters)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Rhetoric is language designed to persuade through stylistic, emotional, or structural means — it may resemble an argument but does not require logical validity or factual grounding. Maaike contrasts rhetoric with argument: rhetoric exhibits indifference to truth, while argument requires ground truth. LLMs, she argues, generate rhetoric — not argument.
