# Least collaborative effort

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (grounding-in-communication)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Least collaborative effort is Clark's principle that communication partners minimize the total effort required to achieve mutual understanding — choosing the most efficient grounding strategy for the medium and context. It is a refinement of (rather than counter to) the cooperative principle: cooperation is not maximal effort but optimal effort.
