# Stream metaphor

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (garden-as-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
The stream metaphor is the publishing paradigm of blogs, social media feeds, and news sites: content flows past in reverse chronological order and effectively disappears. Maaike contrasts it with the digital garden metaphor: the stream is about throughput and recency, the garden is about cultivation and growth.
