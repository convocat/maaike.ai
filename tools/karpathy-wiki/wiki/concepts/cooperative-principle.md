# Cooperative principle

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (why-chatgpt-is-bullshit-and-why-we-should-design-for-that)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
The cooperative principle is Grice's framework for conversational behavior: participants implicitly agree to contribute in ways that serve the conversation's purpose, following four maxims — quality (be truthful), quantity (be informative), relevance (be relevant), and manner (be clear). Maaike uses it to articulate precisely what LLMs violate: they perform the form of cooperative conversation without fulfilling its substance.

## How Maaike uses this concept
The cooperative principle is the implicit contract that the conversation metaphor activates. When users engage with a conversational AI, they bring Gricean expectations — they expect the system to be trying to tell the truth, to say what's useful, to be relevant. LLMs violate especially the maxim of quality (truthfulness) through indifference to truth. This is not a minor technical shortcoming; it is a fundamental breach of the conversational contract users believe they have entered.
