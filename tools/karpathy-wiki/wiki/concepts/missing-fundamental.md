# Missing fundamental

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (llm-hallucinations-knowledge-as-missing-fundamental)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
The missing fundamental is an acoustic phenomenon where the perceived pitch of a sound is absent from the actual signal — the brain infers it from harmonics. Maaike uses it as a metaphor for LLM knowledge: LLM outputs sound grounded and coherent, but the ground truth is missing. You can hear something that isn't there. The perception of knowledge is generated without the knowledge itself being present.

## How Maaike uses this concept
The missing fundamental analogy is one of Maaike's most original conceptual moves. It explains why LLM outputs feel authoritative: they produce all the harmonics of knowledgeable speech (structure, confidence, appropriate vocabulary, logical flow) without containing the fundamental (access to ground truth). Users perceive trustworthiness from the pattern, even when the pattern has no grounding. This is why designing for doubt is necessary — the perception of ground truth is structurally built in.
