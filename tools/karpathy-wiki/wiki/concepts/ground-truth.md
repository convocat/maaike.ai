# Ground truth

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (llm-hallucinations-knowledge-as-missing-fundamental)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Ground truth is the verified, factual state of reality that claims can be measured against. In Maaike's framework, it is the thing LLMs structurally lack access to — and this absence is what makes hallucinations, confabulation, and bullshit behavior unavoidable within the current paradigm. Argument requires ground truth; rhetoric (and bullshit) does not.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Ground truth is the negative space in Maaike's LLM analysis. Almost everything she says about LLMs is structured around what they lack, and ground truth is the central absence. Without access to ground truth, LLMs cannot verify, cannot correct, cannot be honest even if they "wanted" to be. This is not a failure of training or alignment — it is a structural consequence of how LLMs work.
