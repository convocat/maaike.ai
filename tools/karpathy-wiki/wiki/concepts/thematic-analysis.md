# Thematic analysis

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2025 (thematic-analysis-approach-a-fresh)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Thematic analysis is a qualitative research method for identifying patterns and themes in text data. Maaike adopts it both as a tool for analyzing her garden's content (to surface implicit themes across posts) and as a candidate interaction model: if the conversation metaphor breaks down for GenAI, thematic analysis offers a different paradigm for human-AI engagement — iterative, pattern-finding, non-linear.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike's field notes document a three-pass thematic analysis method she developed with Claude for her garden: Pass 1 (extraction), Pass 2 (TAO — themes, attributes, open questions), Pass 3 (synthesis). She identifies seven recurring themes in her work: quest for truth, voice of the maker, community as infrastructure, garden as epistemology, design is not engineering, bilingual practitioner, and knowledge graph as research program. Beyond the garden project, Maaike proposes thematic analysis as an interaction model alternative to conversation — structurally different enough to escape the conversation metaphor's problems.
