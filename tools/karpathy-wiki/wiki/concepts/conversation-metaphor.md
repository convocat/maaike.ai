# Conversation metaphor

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (is-conversation-still-a-useful-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
The conversation metaphor is the framing of human-machine interaction as a conversation: two parties, turn-taking, natural language exchange. Maaike's analysis of this metaphor is central to her body of work: she argues it was always imperfect for chatbots, has become actively misleading for GenAI, and should be challenged by designers who offer better alternatives.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike's critique operates on multiple levels. At the pragmatic level: conversations presuppose grounding, cooperative principles, and situated response — things LLMs cannot deliver. At the design level: the conversation metaphor activates anthropomorphism in users, leading to misplaced trust and misaligned expectations. At the professional level: conversation designers built their entire practice around this metaphor and need to examine whether it still serves. The question "is conversation still a useful metaphor?" is not rhetorical — Maaike genuinely explores whether it should be replaced, and with what.
