# Bullshit

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (why-chatgpt-is-bullshit-and-why-we-should-design-for-that)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Bullshit, in Frankfurt's precise philosophical sense, is speech or output produced without concern for whether it is true or false. The bullshitter is not a liar — a liar knows the truth and conceals it. The bullshitter simply doesn't care. Maaike applies this concept to LLMs with conviction: their mechanism (probabilistic word prediction) makes them structurally indifferent to truth. They are not accidentally wrong; they are constitutionally unconcerned with being right.

## How Maaike uses this concept
The bullshit framing is one of Maaike's sharpest analytical moves. By using Frankfurt's precise definition rather than the colloquial sense, she forces a specific claim: that the problem with LLMs is not accuracy but orientation. You can improve accuracy through RAG, tool use, or retrieval — but you cannot give an LLM an orientation toward truth unless you change its underlying mechanism. This has design implications: you cannot rely on the system to self-correct, you have to design the interface to support user skepticism. The title "Why ChatGPT is bullshit" was deliberately provocative; the article's argument is rigorous.
