# Topic-based writing

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (context-engineering-lets-call-it-design)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Topic-based writing is a technical writing approach that organizes content into self-contained, typed topics (task, concept, reference) that can be reused across contexts. Maaike cites it as another existing structured writing methodology that maps onto context design practice.
