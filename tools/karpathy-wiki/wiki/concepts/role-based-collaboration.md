# Role-based collaboration

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2025 (from-role-to-skills-how-claude-code-desilos-the-future-of-work)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Role-based collaboration is the traditional model of professional work: people in fixed, defined roles (designer, developer, writer, PM) each contributing their specialized function. Maaike argues GenAI, and Claude Code in particular, challenges and disrupts this model by enabling individuals to take on skills across traditional role boundaries.
