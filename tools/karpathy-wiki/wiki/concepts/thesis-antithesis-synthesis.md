# Thesis-antithesis-synthesis

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (thinking-in-action-precision-matters)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Thesis-antithesis-synthesis is Hegel's three-step dialectical process: begin with an initial position (thesis), confront it with a counter-position (antithesis), arrive at a synthesis that integrates both. It is the structured form of dialectical thinking that Maaike advocates as a cognitive practice.
