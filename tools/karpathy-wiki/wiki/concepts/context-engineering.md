# Context engineering

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (context-engineering-lets-call-it-design)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Context engineering is Andrej Karpathy's term for the practice of filling an LLM's context window with precisely the right information for the next step. Maaike engages with this term directly, accepting the underlying activity it describes while arguing that "engineering" is the wrong frame — the work is design, not engineering, and calling it engineering misidentifies who should be doing it.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike uses context engineering as the foil for her own concept of context design. She doesn't dismiss the work Karpathy is describing — she thinks it's important and real. But she argues the engineering label comes with assumptions (about who does it, what skills it requires, what it is) that obscure the structural similarity to information architecture and instructional design. Context engineering is context design by another name, and the name matters.
