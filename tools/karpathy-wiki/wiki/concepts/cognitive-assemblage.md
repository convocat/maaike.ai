# Cognitive assemblage

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (reading-notes-bacteria-to-ai)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Cognitive assemblage is N. Katherine Hayles's concept that cognition is distributed across humans, technologies, and environments — not located in individual minds. Maaike encounters it through reading and uses it to challenge anthropocentric assumptions about AI: LLMs are not a new kind of mind, they are a node in a long-running cognitive assemblage.
