# Delegation metaphor

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (is-conversation-still-a-useful-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
The delegation metaphor frames human-AI interaction as task assignment: the human briefs an agent, specifies a deliverable, and receives the output — without ongoing turn-taking or cooperative exchange. Maaike offers it as an alternative to the conversation metaphor that better fits agentic AI and task-oriented GenAI use.

## How Maaike uses this concept
The delegation metaphor reframes the interaction from a relational to a contractual register. You are not chatting with an AI; you are delegating to one. This has design implications: the interaction is bounded by a brief, a deliverable, and acceptance criteria — not by conversational flow. It also reduces the anthropomorphism risk: you don't form a relationship with a contractor the way you do with a conversational partner.
