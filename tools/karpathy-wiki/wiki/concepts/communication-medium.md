# Communication medium

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (grounding-in-communication)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Communication medium, in Clark's framework, refers to the channel or environment of communication — face-to-face, phone, text, digital interface. The medium shapes grounding strategies: what grounding acts are possible, how much effort they cost, what constraints apply. In Maaike's engagement with this material, it is part of understanding why digital/AI media require adapted grounding strategies.
