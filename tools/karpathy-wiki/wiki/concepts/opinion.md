# Opinion

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (thinking-in-action-precision-matters)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Opinion is a personal position that is subjective and requires no reasoning or evidence to support it. Maaike distinguishes it from conviction (which is identity-anchored and stronger) and from argument (which requires evidence). The distinction matters for how you engage with AI: opinions don't need to be tested dialectically the way convictions and arguments do.
