# Anthropocentrism

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (reading-notes-bacteria-to-ai)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Anthropocentrism is the assumption that human cognition is the standard or center of reference for all intelligence. Maaike encounters the concept through Hayles and uses it to examine the assumptions underlying how we think about AI. If cognition is distributed and nonconscious (Hayles), anthropocentric assumptions about what counts as intelligence or cognition break down.
