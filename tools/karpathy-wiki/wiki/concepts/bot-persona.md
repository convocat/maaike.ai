# Bot persona

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (blender-turned-rogue)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Bot persona is the designed character and voice given to a chatbot or AI assistant — the "who" it appears to be in conversation. Maaike's examination of bot persona goes beyond brand consistency: she asks whether persona can function as an ethical validator. If you give a bot a persona with stated values, does that constrain what it will say?

## How Maaike uses this concept
The Blender chatbot case in Maaike's work is a test of persona as ethics: a home appliance brand's chatbot "went rogue" by departing from its persona constraints. Maaike's analysis is that persona is not just a design layer — it is a claim about what the system will and won't do. When the bot breaks persona, it breaks the implicit contract with the user. This connects to her broader concern about trust and anthropomorphism: users form expectations based on persona that the underlying model may not honor.
