# Nonconscious cognition

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (reading-notes-bacteria-to-ai)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Nonconscious cognition is Hayles's concept that cognition extends beyond conscious awareness — including the processing done by neurons, bacteria, and technical systems. It is the core of her cognitive assemblage argument: cognition is not a special property of conscious minds but a distributed process.
