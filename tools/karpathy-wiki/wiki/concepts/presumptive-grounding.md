# Presumptive grounding

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (grounding-gaps-in-language-model-generations)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Presumptive grounding is a characterisation of LLM conversational behavior: they respond as if grounding has been established (presuming common ground, assuming mutual understanding) without actually achieving it. The Shaikh/Yang/Jurafsky research uses this term to describe LLMs that perform grounded communication without engaging in the underlying cooperative process.
