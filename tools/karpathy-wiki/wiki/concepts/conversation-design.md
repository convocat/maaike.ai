# Conversation design

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2022 (conversational-interfaces-are-not-easy)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Conversation design is the discipline of designing interactions between humans and conversational interfaces — scripting, flow, persona, and error handling. It is Maaike's professional home territory. Her analysis of LLMs and conversational AI is shaped by years of practice in this field, and her critique of the conversation metaphor is partly a self-critique: what does it mean for conversation designers when the metaphor they built their practice on is inadequate?

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike speaks about conversation design from the inside. Her arguments about the conversation metaphor, anthropomorphism, and designing for doubt are addressed partly to her own professional community. She holds conversation design accountable: the discipline needs to examine whether its default patterns (warmth, persona, relational tone) serve users well in the GenAI era, or whether they amplify the anthropomorphism and indifference-to-truth problems.
