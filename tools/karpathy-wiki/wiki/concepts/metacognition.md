# Metacognition

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (is-conversation-still-a-useful-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Metacognition is thinking about one's own thinking: awareness, monitoring, and regulation of cognitive processes. Maaike uses it in two registers: as a better metaphor for human-AI interaction (inner dialog as metacognition-in-action), and as a property that good knowledge practices (digital gardens, dialectical thinking) cultivate.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike sees metacognition as both a design target and a practice to protect. As a design target: AI interaction could be framed as a form of metacognitive scaffolding — the system helps you think about your thinking, not just do your thinking for you. As a practice to protect: the digital garden, public thinking, and dialectical challenge are all metacognitive practices that GenAI can erode if it produces polished outputs instead of supporting genuine reflection.
