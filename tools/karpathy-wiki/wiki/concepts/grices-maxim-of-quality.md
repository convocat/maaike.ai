# Grice's maxim of quality

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (why-chatgpt-is-bullshit-and-why-we-should-design-for-that)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Grice's maxim of quality is the conversational norm stating that speakers should only assert what they believe to be true and have adequate evidence for. It is the maxim that LLMs systematically violate through their indifference to truth. Maaike uses it as the specific point of failure in the conversation metaphor: users expect quality from conversational partners, and LLMs cannot deliver it.
