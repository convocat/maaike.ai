# Indifference to truth

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (why-chatgpt-is-bullshit-and-why-we-should-design-for-that)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Indifference to truth is Frankfurt's core property of bullshit: producing outputs without any concern for whether they are true or false. Maaike uses it as the precise epistemic characterisation of LLMs — not that they are wrong, but that truth is simply not a parameter in their generation process. It is incompatible with conversational grounding and is structurally reinforced by the probabilistic word prediction mechanism.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Indifference to truth is the thread connecting Maaike's arguments across multiple articles. It is the property that makes LLMs bullshitters (Frankfurt), that violates Grice's maxim of quality, that makes hallucinations structurally unavoidable, and that makes the conversation metaphor dangerous. Cognitive laziness reinforces indifference to truth from the user side — users don't verify because verification is cognitively costly. Designing for doubt is the design response.
