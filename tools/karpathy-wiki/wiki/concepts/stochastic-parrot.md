# Stochastic parrot

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (why-chatgpt-is-bullshit-and-why-we-should-design-for-that)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Stochastic parrot is Bender and Gebru's characterisation of LLMs: systems that generate plausible-sounding text by stochastically stitching together patterns from training data, without any understanding of meaning. Maaike uses this as a complementary lens to her Frankfurt-based bullshit framing — both point to the same structural absence in LLMs, from different disciplinary angles.

## How Maaike uses this concept
The stochastic parrot framing is useful for Maaike because it comes from within computational linguistics and speaks to the question of meaning: LLMs do not understand language, they pattern-match it. This is why they confabulate — they produce outputs that are linguistically coherent but semantically ungrounded. The parroting metaphor captures the mimicry without understanding.
