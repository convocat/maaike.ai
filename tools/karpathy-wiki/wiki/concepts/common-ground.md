# Common ground

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (grounding-in-communication)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Common ground is the mutually known, believed, and presupposed information that participants share in a conversation — theorised by Herbert Clark and Susan Brennan. It is both a precondition for and a product of conversational grounding. LLMs cannot build or maintain common ground across conversations in the way humans do.
