# Emotional labor

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (is-conversation-still-a-useful-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Emotional labor is the management of feeling to create a publicly observable expression — a concept originally from sociology (Hochschild) applied here to chat agents that are designed to perform relational and emotional work. Helena Rodemann argues that personality in chat agents constitutes a form of emotional labor. Maaike uses this to highlight the ethical and design implications of building warmth and relatability into AI systems.
