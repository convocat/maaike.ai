# Thinking in public

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (garden-as-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Thinking in public is the practice of sharing unfinished, evolving thinking rather than only polished conclusions. Maaike positions this as a core value of her digital garden and a practice under threat from GenAI, which produces finished-seeming outputs. The garden's maturity system makes thinking-in-public structural rather than accidental.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike's commitment to thinking in public is both philosophical and practical. Philosophically: finished outputs conceal the thinking process; rough notes expose it. The garden's value is in showing how ideas develop, not just what they conclude. Practically: she uses the maturity system (draft, developing, solid, complete) to make the state of thinking visible on every page. GenAI poses a specific threat here — not because it thinks for you, but because its outputs look finished even when the thinking behind them is shallow.
