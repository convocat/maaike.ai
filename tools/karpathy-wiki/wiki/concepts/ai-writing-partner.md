# AI as writing partner

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (ai-as-writing-partner)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
AI as writing partner is a framing of the human-AI relationship in which the AI functions as a collaborative co-author — not a tool that executes instructions, but a partner that contributes ideas, suggests framings, and pushes back. Maaike examines this framing with ambivalence: she values the externalizing function of AI in the writing process, but worries about the disappearance of authentic voice when AI fills the blank page.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike's concern is not that AI helps with writing but that the collaboration model shifts where the authentic thinking happens. If AI generates the first draft, the human's role becomes editorial — which is a fundamentally different cognitive posture than generative writing. The inner dialog that writing makes visible (a key value in her garden philosophy) is disrupted when the AI colonizes that space. The "writing partner" metaphor may be more honest than "AI assistant," but it still papers over this tension.
