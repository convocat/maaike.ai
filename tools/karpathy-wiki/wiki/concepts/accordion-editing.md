# Accordion editing

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (conversational-interfaces-are-not-easy)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Accordion editing is Nielsen Norman Group's term for the AI writing behavior of iteratively shortening or lengthening generated text through prompts — squeezing and expanding like an accordion. Maaike adopts it as one of two key observed user behaviors (with apple picking) that reveal the usability problems of conversational AI for writing tasks.
