# Umwelt

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (reading-notes-bacteria-to-ai)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Umwelt (from Jakob von Uexküll) is the concept that each organism has a unique subjective world defined by what it can perceive and act upon. It appears in Maaike's reading notes as a concept incompatible with anthropocentrism: if every organism has its own Umwelt, the human one is not the standard.
