# Context design

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (context-engineering-lets-call-it-design)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Context design is Maaike's reframing of "context engineering" — Andrej Karpathy's term for the practice of filling an LLM's context window with precisely the right information for the next step. Maaike argues that this work is structurally identical to what information architects, instructional designers, and technical authors have always done: designing the information environment that shapes understanding and behavior. Renaming it "design" is not cosmetic — it signals who belongs at the table and what expertise is relevant.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Context design is Maaike's most programmatic concept: it is the design discipline she is proposing and advocating for. Her argument is that LLM practitioners who focus on prompt engineering are reinventing wheels that information design disciplines have been turning for decades. Semantic information types, structured content, typed blocks — these are tools designers and authors already have. The GenAI revolution needs them, but doesn't know it yet. Context design is the frame that makes that expertise visible.
