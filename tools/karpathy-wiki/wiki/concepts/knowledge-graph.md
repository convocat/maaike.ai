# Knowledge graph

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2025 (knowledge-graph-for-the-garden)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
A knowledge graph is a structured representation of entities and their relationships — nodes connected by typed edges. In Maaike's work, it is both a research project (building a semantic knowledge graph for her digital garden) and a metaphor for how she understands knowledge itself: not as a hierarchy of topics but as a web of relationships. The garden-as-knowledge-graph is one of her seven identified themes.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike's knowledge graph project is one of the most technically involved projects in her garden: it involves key phrase extraction, embedding models, similarity thresholds, and typed relations. The goal is to surface connections between posts that aren't explicitly linked — to automate a form of serendipitous discovery. This wiki is itself a knowledge graph artifact: named entities, concepts, relationships expressed as predicates.
