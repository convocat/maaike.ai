# Cognitive load

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (conversational-interfaces-are-not-easy)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Cognitive load is the mental effort required to process information and complete a task. Maaike uses it to explain why conversational interfaces are poorly suited to non-linear work: they force everything into a linear, linguistic format, imposing high cognitive load on users who are trying to do non-linear, spatial, or iterative tasks.
