# Digital garden

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (garden-as-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
A digital garden is a non-linear, evolving personal knowledge space — contrasted with the stream metaphor of blogging and social media, where content flows past and disappears. Maaike has built her own digital garden (maaike.ai) and written about the metaphor both as a practical choice and as an epistemological stance: tending knowledge publicly, letting ideas develop over time rather than publishing finished thoughts.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike's relationship to the digital garden is deeply personal and practical — she built one, maintains it, and writes about the process. Her garden is structured around a maturity system (draft to complete) that makes thinking-in-public visible. She also uses the garden as a lens on GenAI: the garden's value is in exposing the thinking process, which GenAI risks short-circuiting by producing polished outputs. The garden metaphor also connects to her broader interest in knowledge infrastructure and serendipitous discovery.
