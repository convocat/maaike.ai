# Argument

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (thinking-in-action-precision-matters)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
An argument is a logical structure consisting of premises, reasoning, and a conclusion — it builds a case in one direction without inherent claim to factual truth, but it does require ground truth as its evidentiary basis. Maaike contrasts argument with rhetoric and opinion: argument can be evaluated against evidence; rhetoric and opinion cannot be challenged in the same way.
