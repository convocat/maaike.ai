# Instructional design

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2024 (context-engineering-lets-call-it-design)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Instructional design is the systematic design of learning experiences and materials, using structured content types to support understanding and task completion. Maaike cites it as one of the existing disciplines whose vocabulary and methods translate directly into context design — evidence that context engineering is not new, just newly named.
