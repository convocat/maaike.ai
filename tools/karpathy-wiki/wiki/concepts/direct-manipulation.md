# Direct manipulation

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (conversational-interfaces-are-not-easy)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Direct manipulation is Shneiderman's interaction design principle: users should be able to act directly on objects of interest rather than through indirect commands or prompts. Maaike invokes it as the design counter to the articulation barrier: instead of asking users to express what they want in language, interfaces should let them act on the content directly.
