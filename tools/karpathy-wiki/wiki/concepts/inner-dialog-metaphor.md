# Inner dialog metaphor

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (is-conversation-still-a-useful-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
The inner dialog metaphor frames human-AI interaction as an extension of your own internal thinking — a metacognitive dialog with yourself, scaffolded by an AI. It reframes the AI not as an external conversational partner but as a form of structured inner monologue made interactive. Maaike proposes it as an alternative to the conversation metaphor that reduces anthropomorphism risk and better reflects how GenAI can serve reflective thinking.

## How Maaike uses this concept
The inner dialog metaphor is Maaike's most philosophically interesting alternative to conversation. Where delegation is contractual and note-taking is instrumental, inner dialog is cognitive: the AI helps you think through your own thinking. This connects directly to metacognition and her interest in thinking in public. It also sidesteps the cooperative expectation problem — in an inner dialog, you don't expect the other voice to be truthful in a Gricean sense; you expect it to be useful for thinking.
