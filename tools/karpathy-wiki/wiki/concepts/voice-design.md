# Voice design

**Type:** design-discipline
**First seen:** 2023 (conversational-interfaces-are-not-easy)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Voice design is the practice of designing spoken conversational interfaces — voice assistants, IVR systems, voice-first experiences. It sits adjacent to conversation design but specializes in the constraints and affordances of speech: prosody, SSML, error recovery in audio-only contexts. Maaike's professional background includes voice design, making her comparisons between text-chat and voice interaction grounded in practice.

## How Maaike uses this concept
Maaike references voice design to frame what conversation designers know that text-focused GenAI practitioners often don't: that the absence of visual cues, the irreversibility of spoken utterances, and the difficulty of error correction in voice interfaces have all been design problems long before LLMs. The conversational interface is not a new design problem — it has a history.
