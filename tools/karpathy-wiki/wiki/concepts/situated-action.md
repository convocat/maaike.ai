# Situated action

**Type:** concept
**First seen:** 2023 (is-conversation-still-a-useful-metaphor)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

## Summary
Situated action is Suchman's concept that human action is shaped by the concrete, unfolding situation rather than by pre-formed abstract plans. In Maaike's work it appears as one of the things LLMs lack: they do not respond to the situation as it unfolds, they process tokens according to learned patterns. The absence of situated action is part of why the conversation metaphor fails.
